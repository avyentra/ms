Privacy Policy

Your information, handled simply

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit ms.primeznews.com or contact me, and how it is used. The short version: this website has no contact form, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics tracking. I only get your details if you choose to send them to me.

Who I am

This website belongs to Mangat Singh, a UI/UX and product designer based in Chandigarh, India. For any privacy question, email rwd.mangat@gmail.com.

Information you send me

When you contact me by email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Upwork, I receive what you choose to share. This is usually your name, email address or phone number, company name and details about your project. Files you send, such as briefs, screenshots or brand assets, are also included.

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • No cookies or tracking: I do not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels or similar tracking on this website.

How I use your information

  • To reply to your message and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes, deliver design work and send invoices.
  • To keep basic records of projects and payments.

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. I do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

Sharing

I share your information only when it's needed for the work. For example, a developer working on your project may see your brief, or a payment platform like Upwork handles a payment. I may also share it when the law requires.

Other websites

This site links to Behance, LinkedIn, Upwork, WhatsApp, Google Maps reviews and articles on other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for how they handle your data.

How long I keep it

I keep project emails and files for as long as they are needed for the work, for support after delivery and for my business and tax records. You can ask me to delete them sooner (see below).

Your choices and rights

You can ask me to show, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email rwd.mangat@gmail.com. I will reply within a reasonable time, in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
